There are many types of dosa. Common ones are plain (sada) dosa, masala dosa with a spiced potato filling, ghee roast, paper roast, rava dosa made with semolina, set dosa that is soft and spongy, and podi dosa with a spiced powder. Which ones a restaurant serves varies, so check its menu.

## Crisp dosas

Ghee roast is cooked crisp with ghee. Paper roast is very thin, large and extra crisp.

## Soft dosas

Set dosa is a stack of small, soft, spongy dosas, usually served with a gravy or chutney.

## Filled and flavoured dosas

Masala dosa has a spiced potato filling. Podi dosa is spread with a spiced lentil and chilli powder.

## Rava dosa

Rava dosa is made with semolina and is lacy and crisp.
