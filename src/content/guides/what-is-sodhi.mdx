Sodhi is a mild, fragrant, coconut-milk based vegetable gravy from Tirunelveli (Nellai) cooking. It is usually eaten with idli, idiyappam, parotta or rice. It is light rather than hot.

## What it goes with

Idli, idiyappam, parotta and rice are common pairings.

## Sodhi at Brihadhee

The kitchen will confirm how sodhi is served at Brihadhee.
