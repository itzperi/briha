A mini tiffin is a small-portion plate of several South Indian tiffin items served together, so you can taste more than one dish in a single order. Typical combinations may include items such as idli, pongal, vada, a sweet and a dosa.

## What tiffin means

In Tamil Nadu, tiffin means the lighter meals eaten at breakfast or in the evening, such as idli, dosa, vada and pongal.

## Who a mini tiffin suits

It suits anyone who wants to taste several items without ordering full portions of each.
